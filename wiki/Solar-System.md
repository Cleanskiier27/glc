# The Solar System

[← Back to Wiki Home](README.md)

## Overview

The Solar System consists of the Sun and everything bound to it by gravity:
eight planets, five recognized dwarf planets, dozens of moons, and countless
asteroids, comets, and other small bodies.

## The Sun

A G-type main-sequence star that contains about 99.8% of the Solar System's
mass. Nuclear fusion in its core converts hydrogen into helium, releasing the
energy that powers life on Earth.

## The Planets

| Planet | Type | Notable Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Mercury** | Terrestrial | Smallest planet; extreme temperature swings |
| **Venus** | Terrestrial | Hottest planet due to a runaway greenhouse effect |
| **Earth** | Terrestrial | The only known planet with life |
| **Mars** | Terrestrial | Target of extensive robotic exploration; candidate for future human missions |
| **Jupiter** | Gas giant | Largest planet; strong magnetic field, Great Red Spot storm |
| **Saturn** | Gas giant | Famous for its extensive ring system |
| **Uranus** | Ice giant | Rotates on its side (~98° axial tilt) |
| **Neptune** | Ice giant | Farthest known planet; strongest winds in the Solar System |

## Dwarf Planets

Pluto, Ceres, Haumea, Makemake, and Eris are officially classified as dwarf
planets — bodies that orbit the Sun and are massive enough to be roughly
round, but have not cleared their orbital neighborhood of other debris.

## Moons and Small Bodies

- **Moons** — Over 300 confirmed moons orbit planets in the Solar System.
  Notable examples include Earth's Moon, Jupiter's Europa (a candidate for
  subsurface oceans), and Saturn's Titan (with a thick atmosphere).
- **Asteroid Belt** — Located between Mars and Jupiter, containing millions
  of rocky bodies.
- **Kuiper Belt** — A region beyond Neptune containing icy bodies, including
  Pluto.
- **Oort Cloud** — A distant, spherical shell of icy objects believed to be
  the source of many long-period comets.
