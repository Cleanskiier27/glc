# Space Agencies

[← Back to Wiki Home](README.md)

Space exploration is carried out by a mix of government agencies and private
companies around the world.

## Government Agencies

| Agency | Country/Region | Founded | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NASA** | United States | 1958 | Led Apollo, Space Shuttle, ISS, and Artemis programs |
| **Roscosmos** | Russia | 1992 (successor to Soviet program) | Operates Soyuz spacecraft, ISS partner |
| **ESA** (European Space Agency) | Europe (22 member states) | 1975 | Operates Ariane rockets, contributes to ISS and Artemis |
| **JAXA** | Japan | 2003 | Operates Kibo module on ISS, Hayabusa asteroid missions |
| **CNSA** (China National Space Administration) | China | 1993 | Operates Tiangong space station, Chang'e lunar missions |
| **ISRO** (Indian Space Research Organisation) | India | 1969 | Chandrayaan lunar missions, Mars Orbiter Mission |
| **CSA** (Canadian Space Agency) | Canada | 1989 | ISS partner, robotics (Canadarm) |

## Private Companies

| Company | Founded | Notable Work |
|---|---|---|
| **SpaceX** | 2002 | Falcon 9/Heavy, Crew Dragon, Starship |
| **Blue Origin** | 2000 | New Shepard suborbital flights, New Glenn rocket |
| **Virgin Galactic** | 2004 | Suborbital space tourism |
| **Rocket Lab** | 2006 | Electron small-satellite launch vehicle |
| **Boeing** | — | CST-100 Starliner crew capsule |

## Collaboration

Many missions, most notably the **International Space Station**, are the
result of collaboration between multiple agencies. This international
cooperation allows for shared costs, combined expertise, and joint scientific
research.
